About BRSpeech-TTS

BRSpeech-TTS is a high-quality, multi-speaker speech synthesis (Text-to-Speech) corpus in Portuguese, developed at the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA) of the Federal University of Goiás (UFG). It is an enhanced version of the Portuguese subset of the CML-TTS Dataset that underwent professional studio-level audio restoration and speech enhancement using Adobe Podcast Enhance (Adobe Speech Enhancement).

Key Enhancements over Original CML-TTS Portuguese:
  • Adobe Speech Enhancement: All recordings from the CML-TTS Portuguese subset were processed through Adobe Enhancement to remove background noise, room reverberation, and microphone artifacts while preserving speaker timbre and prosody.
  • Full-Bandwidth 48 kHz Audio: Upsampled and restored to 48 kHz (16-bit PCM Mono FLAC/WAV), compared to 24 kHz in the original CML-TTS release.
  • ASR-Verified Transcriptions: Segments were transcribed using OpenAI Whisper Large-v2 and MMS, aligned against public-domain literary texts from Project Gutenberg and LibriVox, achieving a mean Levenshtein similarity score of 0.983 (median 1.000).
  • Verified Per-Speaker Accent Labels (pt_br vs. pt_eu): Every clip across all 62 speakers was evaluated with the lgris/portuguese-accent-classifier model, identifying 51 Brazilian Portuguese (pt_br) speakers (158.75 h / 99.30%) and 11 European Portuguese (pt_eu) speakers (1.12 h / 0.70%).

Dataset Statistics & Analysis

BRSpeech-TTS comprises recordings of 55 public-domain literary works from Project Gutenberg read by 62 volunteer narrators from the LibriVox project: 51 Brazilian Portuguese (pt_br) speakers accounting for 158.75 hours (99.30%) of total duration, and 11 European Portuguese (pt_eu) speakers accounting for 1.12 hours (0.70%). Across the 39,230 base FLAC recordings (20.59 GB compressed FLAC; further segmented into 76,322 utterances in the Hugging Face Hub release), the corpus totals 159.87 hours of speech and 1,306,913 words (7.61 million characters).

2. Portuguese Accent Classification (pt_br vs. pt_eu)

To provide accurate dialect labels for TTS training and accent-conditioned synthesis, all 39,230 base FLAC files across the 62 speakers were classified using lgris/portuguese-accent-classifier (checkpoint 4f287aba669b7688570a213bdab125631a0a0920) using sliding 5.0 s windows (2.5 s hop) at 16 kHz. Each speaker's accent label (pt_br or pt_eu) is determined by majority vote across all of their utterances (with 0 classification errors across all 39,230 clips). The full per-speaker table is available in accent_por_locutor.csv and integrated into the accent column of the Hugging Face Hub dataset.
