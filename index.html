About BRSpeech-TTS

BRSpeech-TTS is a high-quality, multi-speaker speech synthesis (Text-to-Speech) corpus in Portuguese, developed at the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA) of the Federal University of Goiás (UFG). It is an enhanced version of the Portuguese subset of the CML-TTS Dataset that underwent professional studio-level audio restoration and speech enhancement using Adobe Podcast Enhance (Adobe Speech Enhancement).

Key Enhancements over Original CML-TTS Portuguese:
  • Adobe Speech Enhancement: All recordings from the CML-TTS Portuguese subset were processed through Adobe Enhancement to remove background noise, room reverberation, and microphone artifacts while preserving speaker timbre and prosody.
  • Full-Bandwidth 48 kHz Audio: Upsampled and restored to 48 kHz (16-bit PCM Mono FLAC/WAV), compared to 24 kHz in the original CML-TTS release.
  • ASR-Verified Transcriptions: Segments were transcribed using OpenAI Whisper Large-v2 and MMS, aligned against public-domain literary texts from Project Gutenberg and LibriVox, achieving a mean Levenshtein similarity score of 0.983 (median 1.000).

Download & Hugging Face Hub

The BRSpeech-TTS dataset is officially hosted on the Hugging Face Hub at https://huggingface.co/datasets/freds0/BRSpeech-TTS/ under the CC-BY 4.0 license. You can load it directly in Python using the Hugging Face datasets library: 

from datasets import load_dataset

# Load the BRSpeech-TTS dataset from Hugging Face Hub
dataset = load_dataset("freds0/BRSpeech-TTS")

print(dataset)

Dataset Statistics & Analysis

BRSpeech-TTS comprises recordings of 55 public-domain literary works from Project Gutenberg read by 62 volunteer narrators from the LibriVox project (61 Brazilian Portuguese speakers accounting for 99.8% of total duration, and 1 European Portuguese speaker in the validation split accounting for 0.2%). Across the 39,230 base FLAC recordings (20.59 GB compressed FLAC; further segmented into 76,322 utterances in the Hugging Face Hub release), the corpus totals 159.87 hours of speech and 1,306,913 words (7.61 million characters).

3. Top Speakers Analysis

The 6 most prolific narrators in the training set (2961, 12249, 9351, 10107, 4367, and 5677) account for 150.29 hours (94.0%) of the dataset, making BRSpeech-TTS suitable for both single-speaker high-resource TTS (e.g., Speaker 2961 with 83.20 hours) and multi-speaker / zero-shot voice cloning across all 62 speakers.
